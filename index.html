01 / About

I started by drawing screens. Now I design the rules behind them.

Product Designer · Hanoi

My first work was mobile UI/UX: a driver app, and two connected apps for MB Bank. Over time it moved upstream, from how a screen looks to how a feature should behave.

Today I design a retail and F&B management platform, where one feature often touches the POS, the back office and the mobile app at once. That taught me to ask what problem we are solving before deciding what the screen looks like.

Outside work I'm usually trying a new AI tool or workflow, which is how I ended up with AI certificates from Anthropic and Google. The rest of the time I'm at the gym or playing sports.

04 / Approach · 6 steps

First I get the requirement right with Product and BA. The screens come after the logic is agreed.

Step 1

Clarify the brief

Read the ticket and business rules, then settle open questions with the PO and BA before drawing.

with PO · BA
Step 2

Map flows & states

Happy path first, then the edge cases and errors a real shop will run into.

Step 3

Wireframe the logic

Low-fidelity screens to agree how the feature behaves before any visual polish.

Step 4

Prototype fast

Clickable prototypes built with AI help, so a flow can be checked early.

AI-assisted
Step 5

Hand off & design QA

Walk Dev and QA through the logic, review test cases, check the build and log UI issues.

with Dev · QA
Step 6

Follow up after release

Track feedback and bug reports after launch, then fix what still gets in the way in the next iteration.

08 / FAQ

Common questions, answered briefly.

What kind of role are you looking for?

Product Designer roles on complex, multi-platform products, where design starts from business rules and flows rather than screens. I am based in Hanoi.

Can I see the work that is under NDA?

The case studies use rebuilt flows, sample data and removed logos. I can walk through the details in an interview, within what the NDA allows.

How do you work with PO, BA and developers?

I clarify requirements with the PO and BA before drawing and wireframe the logic first. Then I walk developers and QA through it, review test cases and check the build before release.

How do you use AI in your work?

To summarise requirements, list edge cases, explore variants and build clickable prototypes faster. Business rules come from Product and BA, and the design decisions stay with me.

Do you work onsite or remotely?

Both. I worked onsite with MB Bank's team as one of three designers, and remotely with overseas clients as a freelancer.

How do you validate a design?

Through requirement reviews with the PO and BA, design QA on builds and feedback after release. Short sessions with real users are the next step I push for when access allows.

Which tools do you use?

Figma and FigJam for design and flows, Figma Variables and Tokens Studio for design tokens, Adobe Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects) for visuals and motion, and Claude for requirements and prototyping.

How can I contact you?

Email me at dtruongdesign.forwork@gmail.com or send a message on LinkedIn. Both links are in the contact section below.
